# Game vision

## Core fantasy

The player freely builds and rides an impossible rollercoaster between real astronomical worlds. The first emotional hook is the journey itself: choose a spectacular ready-made shape or tune a personal route, leave a familiar Earth, look around from a carriage in open space, watch the destination grow from its true apparent size, and arrive somewhere that felt impossibly distant.

The railway is fictional adaptive technology. Surrounding scale, apparent body sizes, broad orbital motion and named measurements remain scientifically grounded enough that the fiction feels anchored. When science and playability conflict, document the compromise and preserve wonder and comprehensibility.

## Experience principles

- The ride is the reward: views, scale, motion and anticipation matter more than menus.
- Creative construction is immediate: no currency, cargo delivery, waiting or orbital-alignment gate stands between choosing a shape and riding it.
- Destinations begin as they really appear and grow naturally during approach.
- Players can look back at Earth, forward to the Moon and sideways into space, with enough rail and carriage structure to communicate movement.
- Fiction has consistent rules; the adaptive rail can change visual gauge and follow an impossible route, but is never described as a viable orbital transfer.
- Fast testing coexists with scenic travel through 1×, 4× and 16× speeds and seeking.
- Desktop is an immersive experience while track-following first person with four clear facing directions, 360° third-person orbit and a movable compact HUD make modern phones a first-class playtest target.

## Scientific boundary

Mean Earth/Moon radii and separation, geometric apparent diameter and a simplified circular lunar orbit are authoritative model inputs. Four simulated days pass during the three-minute ride. The fixed cubic rail aims at the Moon's future position and arrives 25 km above its near side. Inclination, eccentricity, libration, solar illumination changes and rotating reference frames are omitted. The rail, its visibility scale, carriage scale, propulsion and accelerated travel are explicit fiction.

## Scope

The current milestone is a complete creative Earth-to-Moon coaster with both cameras, visible track, seven templates, fine-tuning, persistent blueprints, telemetry and arrival/restart. The implemented freight/economy prototype is dormant and retained only for rollback. Near-term work improves creative construction, journey credibility, comfort, visual staging, accessibility, audio and measured performance before adding more worlds.
